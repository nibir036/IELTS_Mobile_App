Describe a city you have visited
  • which city it was
  • when you went there
  • what you did
  • and how you felt about it

I would like to talk about a trip I made to Kolkata a couple of years ago, which left a lasting impression on me. It is an old, historic city, and what struck me immediately was its wonderful sense of character. The streets were bustling with activity, full of markets, trams and street food that smelled incredible. What I loved most was the blend of old and new; grand colonial buildings stood alongside modern shops, creating a genuinely atmospheric feel. I spent my days exploring museums and wandering through the older neighbourhoods, which felt almost timeless. The people were remarkably friendly and always happy to point me in the right direction. Of course, it was crowded and chaotic at times, but that energy was part of its charm. Honestly, I found the whole experience genuinely invigorating, and it gave me a real appreciation for the city's rich cultural heritage. I would go back without hesitation.

Key vocabulary: bustling · atmospheric · timeless · invigorating
