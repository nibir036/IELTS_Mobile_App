Describe a gift you received that was important to you
  • what it was
  • who gave it to you
  • when you received it
  • and why it was important

A gift that means a great deal to me is a fountain pen my father gave me when I finished my studies. It is not particularly expensive, but its sentimental value is enormous. He handed it to me with a short note saying he hoped I would use it to sign many important documents in my career. What made it so special was the thoughtfulness behind it, rather than the object itself. My father is not a man of many words, so this gesture was genuinely touching. I keep it safely on my desk and use it only on special occasions. Every time I hold it, I am reminded of his quiet faith in me. Honestly, it is the kind of gift that grows more precious with time. I think the best presents are those that carry meaning and memory, not just a high price tag. This pen is something I intend to keep for the rest of my life and perhaps pass on one day.

Key vocabulary: sentimental · thoughtfulness · touching · precious
