Describe a person who has influenced you
  • who the person is
  • how you know them
  • what they did
  • and how they influenced you

The person who has influenced me most is my father, who ran a small business for over thirty years. Growing up, I watched him deal with setbacks that would have discouraged most people, yet he always stayed calm and kept going. What shaped me was not any single piece of advice, but the quiet example he set day after day. He treated every customer, however difficult, with the same integrity, and he taught me that a good reputation is built slowly and lost quickly. When I was struggling to choose a career, he never pushed me in one direction; instead he encouraged me to be self-reliant and make my own decisions. Looking back, almost everything I value about hard work and honesty can be traced to him. His influence has been profound, and I still catch myself asking what he would do in a tricky situation. In many ways he remains my quiet role model, even now that I am an adult.

Key vocabulary: discouraged · integrity · self-reliant · profound

It reminded me that spending time with a child forces you to slow down and be fully present, something adults rarely manage. Honestly, that afternoon was a welcome escape from my usual routine, and I have made a point of seeing him more often since.
