Category 1 — Personal & Background

1
Hometown
Where is your hometown?

I'm from Sirajganj, a modest town in the north of Bangladesh, right beside the Jamuna river. It's about four hours from Dhaka by road, and I've lived there most of my life, so it has a certain tranquillity that feels like home.

2
Home
Do you live in a house or a flat?

I live in a flat, on the third floor of a building in a quiet residential area. It's a three-bedroom place I share with my family. It suits us well, though I do occasionally wish we had a bit more open space around us.

3
Family
Can you tell me about your family?

It's a fairly typical family. There are four of us: my parents, my younger sister and me. My father runs a small business and my mother looks after the home. We're quite close-knit, and we usually have dinner together every night.

4
Work
Do you work or are you a student?

I'm working at the moment, at an education consultancy, where I predominantly help students with their applications abroad. It keeps me busy, but seeing someone reach a goal they worked towards is genuinely rewarding, so the effort feels worthwhile.

5
Studies
What subject did you study?

I studied business administration at university, with a focus on management. It gave me a broad understanding of how organisations actually run, which has proved surprisingly invaluable in the work I do now.

6
Your name
Does your name have any special meaning?

Yes, it does actually. My name carries a meaning connected to guidance and light, which is fairly common in our culture. I quite like it, because names here are usually chosen with real deliberation behind them.

7
Daily routine
What is your typical daily routine?

It's fairly structured on weekdays. I get up around seven, have a quick breakfast, and I'm at work by nine. After work I usually study for a couple of hours, then unwind before bed. Weekends are far more relaxed.

8
Neighbours
Do you know your neighbours well?

Reasonably well, yes. In our building people are fairly friendly, so we greet each other and occasionally chat. I wouldn't say we're close, but there's a warm, cordial relationship, which matters when you live so near people.

9
Friends
Do you have many friends?

I wouldn't say many, but I have a small circle I really trust. I've always preferred a few close friendships over a wide range of acquaintances. Those are the people I can be completely myself around.

10
Childhood
What did you enjoy doing as a child?

I was quite an outdoorsy kid. I'd spend hours playing cricket with the neighbourhood children until it got dark. Looking back, those were some of the most carefree times of my life.

11
Hometown changes
Has your hometown changed much in recent years?

Quite a lot, actually. There are more shops, better roads and far more traffic than when I was younger. Some of it is welcome progress, but part of me misses how quiet and unspoilt it used to feel.

12
City vs countryside
Do you prefer living in a city or the countryside?

That's a tough one. I enjoy the energy of a city, but I genuinely appreciate the calm of the countryside. Honestly, the ideal for me would be a quieter town that isn't too far from a city.

13
Favourite room
Which room do you spend the most time in?

Definitely my bedroom, which doubles as my study, so I'm in there for most of the day when I'm home. I've arranged it to be quiet and comfortable, which makes it easy to concentrate.

14
Languages
What languages do you speak?

I speak Bengali as my mother tongue, and English fairly fluently. I picked up English mostly through school and a lot of self-study, and I use it daily at work, so it's become almost instinctive.

15
Future plans
What are your plans for the future?

In the short term, I'm focused on strengthening my career and possibly studying abroad. Longer term, I'd like to reach a point where my work has real impact and I have the stability to enjoy life a little more.

16
A typical day
Which part of the day do you like most?

The early morning, surprisingly. It's quiet, the day hasn't got hectic yet, and I feel clear-headed. A calm start with a cup of tea genuinely sets a productive tone for everything that follows.

17
Weekends
What do you usually do at the weekend?

I try to keep weekends flexible after a busy week. Usually I catch up on sleep, meet a friend or two, and maybe run some errands. If I'm lucky, I'll fit in a book or a film to properly recharge.

18
Sleep
Do you usually sleep well?

Mostly, yes, though it varies. When my mind is calm I fall asleep quickly, but on stressful days it takes a while to switch off. I've learned that avoiding my phone before bed is remarkably effective.

19
Punctuality
Are you a punctual person?

I'd like to think so, yes. I genuinely dislike keeping people waiting, so I usually aim to arrive a little early. To me, being on time is a simple way of showing consideration for others.

20
Best friend
Can you describe your best friend?

Sure. He's easygoing, genuinely kind and a great listener, which is probably why we've stayed close for so long. He has this reassuring way of putting things in perspective whenever I'm stressed.

Category 2 — Lifestyle & Habits

21
Cooking
Do you enjoy cooking?

I do, actually, though I only really cook at the weekend. During the week I'm too busy, but on a lazy Saturday I quite enjoy trying a new recipe. I find the whole process rather therapeutic after a hectic week.

22
Meals
What is your favourite meal of the day?

Definitely dinner. It's the one meal where the whole family sits down together and actually talks, so it's as much about the company as the food. It's become a small daily ritual I really value.

23
Shopping
Do you enjoy shopping?

It depends what I'm shopping for, honestly. I like buying books or a bit of tech, but clothes shopping drains me quickly. These days I do most of it online, which saves me a lot of time.

24
Clothes
What kind of clothes do you like to wear?

I tend to keep it simple and comfortable, mostly casual shirts and jeans. I'm not one for chasing trends. As long as something is neat and versatile, I'm perfectly happy with it.

25
Mobile phones
How often do you use your mobile phone?

Far too often, if I'm honest. It's become almost indispensable for work and staying in touch, so it's rarely out of my hand. Lately I've been trying to set limits, because it's easy to lose track of time.

26
Technology
Do you think you rely too much on technology?

To some extent, yes. It makes life easier, but I've noticed I reach for my phone out of habit rather than need. I'm trying to be more mindful, especially in the evenings when I should be relaxing.

27
Exercise
Do you do any sports or exercise?

I try to, though not as consistently as I should. I go for a brisk walk most evenings, and occasionally I play badminton. I always feel rejuvenated afterwards, so I really ought to do more of it.

28
Healthy living
Do you have a healthy lifestyle?

Reasonably, I'd say. I eat home-cooked food most of the time and try to stay active, but my sleep could be better. It's an ongoing effort, but I think I'm gradually cultivating better habits.

29
Tea or coffee
Do you prefer tea or coffee?

Tea, without a doubt. It's a bit of a ritual here, and I probably have three or four cups a day. Coffee is fine occasionally, but nothing quite beats a hot cup of tea in the morning.

30
Mornings
Do you usually get up early?

Yes, on weekdays I'm up by seven. I've found I think much more clearly in the mornings, so I make the most of that quiet, uninterrupted time. On weekends, though, I happily allow myself a lie-in.

31
Housework
Do you do much housework?

A fair amount, yes. I take care of my own room and help out at the weekend. I can't say I enjoy it, but there's a certain satisfaction in a tidy, well-organised space.

32
Money
Are you good at saving money?

I'm getting better at it. I used to spend fairly carelessly, but now I set aside a fixed amount each month before anything else. Being frugal in small ways gives me real peace of mind.

33
Time management
Are you good at managing your time?

Mostly, yes. I rely a lot on a simple to-do list, which keeps me on track. I'm not perfect, and things occasionally slip, but planning ahead stops me feeling overwhelmed.

34
Habits
Do you have any habits you would like to change?

One or two, definitely. I spend too long on my phone, and I sometimes procrastinate. I'm slowly working on both, though old habits are surprisingly hard to break.

35
Relaxing
How do you relax after a busy day?

Usually with something simple. I'll read, listen to music, or watch an episode of a show. Anything that lets me properly decompress from work does the trick.

36
Public transport
Do you often use public transport?

Quite often, yes, mainly buses and the occasional rickshaw for shorter trips. It's affordable and saves the stress of parking, although it can get congested during peak hours, which is the main downside.

37
Walking
Do you like walking?

I do, more than I expected to. A slow evening walk is one of my favourite ways to unwind, and it's good for my health too. It clears my head far more effectively than sitting in front of a screen.

38
Weather and plans
Does the weather affect your daily plans?

Quite a bit, actually. The rainy season here can be relentless, so I often check the forecast before heading out. On a pleasant, sunny day I'm far more likely to go out and do something active.

39
Routine
Do you prefer having a routine or variety?

A bit of both, ideally. A steady routine keeps me productive, but I'd get bored without some variety. So I like structure on weekdays and something a little more spontaneous at the weekend.

40
Eating out
Do you prefer eating at home or in restaurants?

Mostly at home, to be honest. Home-cooked food is healthier and more comforting. That said, I do enjoy dining out now and then, especially when it's a chance to socialise with friends over a meal.

Category 3 — Interests & Hobbies

41
Free time
What do you usually do in your free time?

It varies with my mood. Often I'll read or listen to music, and if I have more time I'll meet friends or watch a film. I try to spend it on things that genuinely relax me rather than mindless scrolling.

42
Reading
Do you like reading?

Yes, very much. I've been a keen reader since childhood. I mostly read non-fiction these days, especially business or self-improvement, since I enjoy the feeling of broadening my knowledge with each book.

43
Books
What kind of books do you enjoy?

I lean towards non-fiction, particularly psychology and personal growth. Occasionally I'll pick up a good novel to switch off, but generally I prefer something that offers a genuine insight or two.

44
Music
Do you like listening to music?

I do, it's part of my daily life really. I listen while I work, travel and relax. My taste is fairly eclectic, from mellow acoustic tracks to a bit of classical when I need to concentrate.

45
Musical instruments
Can you play a musical instrument?

Not properly, unfortunately. I dabbled with the guitar as a teenager but never persevered. It's one of those things I'd genuinely love to return to one day, when I finally have the time.

46
Sport
What sports do you like?

Cricket, above all, which is almost a national obsession here. I enjoy playing casually, but I love watching it even more. I also play a bit of badminton, which is a fun way to stay active with friends.

47
Watching sport
Do you enjoy watching sports?

Absolutely, especially cricket. There's a real buzz when the national team is playing, and the whole country seems to stop and watch. Even people who aren't usually into sport get caught up in the excitement.

48
Photography
Do you like taking photos?

I do, casually at least. I mostly take photos on my phone when I travel or see something striking. I'm no expert, but I love the idea of preserving a moment so I can look back on it later.

49
Drawing
Are you good at drawing or painting?

Not really, I'll admit. I enjoy looking at art far more than creating it. My drawing skills are fairly rudimentary, so I happily leave that to more talented people.

50
Collecting
Do you collect anything?

Books, if that counts. I find it hard to give any away, so my collection just keeps growing. There's something comforting about a shelf full of books you've read, or still intend to read one day.

51
Games
Do you play any video games?

Occasionally, mostly simple ones on my phone to pass the time. I'm not a serious gamer by any means. For me it's more of a light distraction during a break than a proper hobby.

52
Outdoor activities
Do you enjoy outdoor activities?

Yes, whenever I get the chance. I love being outdoors, whether it's a walk by the river or a short trip somewhere green. Being in nature is easily the best way for me to rejuvenate.

53
Cooking as a hobby
Do you find cooking relaxing?

Surprisingly, yes. When I'm not rushed, following a recipe and getting fully immersed in it takes my mind off everything else. And of course there's the reward of a good meal at the end.

54
Gardening

A little, yes. We keep a few potted plants at home, and I quite enjoy tending to them. There's something calming about nurturing something and watching it grow because of the small effort you put in.

55
Writing
Do you like writing?

I do, actually. My work involves a fair bit of writing, and I've grown to enjoy shaping ideas into clear, coherent sentences. I sometimes jot down thoughts in a notebook too, just for myself.

56
Dancing

Not particularly well. I'll join in at weddings and celebrations, which is impossible to avoid here, but I'd never call myself a dancer. I do it purely for the fun of the moment rather than any real flair.

57
New hobbies
Is there a new hobby you would like to try?

Yes, I'd love to learn photography properly. I already enjoy taking casual shots, so mastering the technical side, like lighting and composition, feels like a natural and exciting progression.

58
Free time amount
Do you have enough free time?

Not really, if I'm honest. Between work and study, my weekdays fill up fast. I safeguard my weekends fiercely, but I do wish I had a little more downtime during the week.

59
Childhood hobbies
Did you have any hobbies as a child?

Plenty. I was always outside playing cricket, and I also loved reading comics and adventure stories. Those simple hobbies kept me endlessly entertained, long before phones and screens took over.

60
Hobbies alone or with others
Do you prefer doing hobbies alone or with others?

It depends on the hobby. Reading and writing I much prefer in solitude, in a quiet corner. But sport and travel are far better shared, because the fun is really in the company.

Category 4 — Entertainment & Culture

61
Films
Do you like watching films?

I do, very much. It's my go-to way to unwind at the weekend. I enjoy a good thriller or a thoughtful drama, and I especially like films that are genuinely thought-provoking long after they've ended.

62
Cinema
Do you prefer watching films at home or at the cinema?

Both have their charm, but I usually watch at home for the comfort. That said, for a big, visually spectacular film, the cinema experience is hard to beat, so I save those for the big screen.

63
Television
Do you watch much television?

Not traditional TV, no. Like a lot of people my age, I've shifted almost entirely to streaming, so I watch what I want, when I want. It gives me far more control over my viewing.

64
TV programmes
What kind of programmes do you like?

I gravitate towards documentaries and the occasional gripping drama. Documentaries in particular appeal to me, because I get to relax and gain a bit of insight at the same time, which feels like time well spent.

65
Social media
Do you use social media a lot?

More than I'd like to admit. I use it to stay in touch and follow the news, but I've noticed how easily an hour vanishes. I'm trying to use it more intentionally rather than out of pure habit.

66
Festivals
What festivals do you celebrate?

The two Eids are the biggest for us, along with cultural events like Pohela Boishakh, the Bengali New Year. They're wonderful occasions, full of food, family and colour, and the whole community becomes vibrant.

67
Celebrations
How do you usually celebrate special occasions?

Usually with family and a large meal, which is really at the heart of any celebration here. We dress up, visit relatives, and the day is loud, lively and warm. Food is always the centrepiece.

68
Art
Are you interested in art?

I appreciate it, though from a distance. I enjoy visiting exhibitions and admiring how much emotion an artist can convey in a single piece, even if I can't create anything like it myself.

69
Museums
Do you like visiting museums?

Yes, actually. I find them fascinating, especially history museums, because they bring the past to life in a way books can't. I don't go nearly often enough, but each visit is genuinely enlightening.

70
Concerts
Have you ever been to a concert?

A few times, yes, mostly local ones. There's a special energy to live music that a recording just can't capture. Being surrounded by a crowd who love the same songs is genuinely exhilarating.

71
Traditional culture
Do you think traditional culture is important?

Very much so. It gives people a sense of identity and connects generations. In a fast-changing world, I think preserving our traditions, our language and festivals especially, matters more than ever.

72
Food culture
Is food an important part of your culture?

Enormously. In Bangladesh, food is at the centre of almost every gathering. Sharing a meal is how we express hospitality, so it's really far more than just eating.

73
Singing
Do you like singing?

I enjoy it, though only in private or among close friends. I wouldn't sing in front of a crowd, but humming along to a favourite song is one of life's simple pleasures.

74
News
Do you follow the news?

Yes, I like to stay informed, mostly through news apps and a bit of online reading. I try not to overdo it, though, because too much news can be draining. A quick daily catch-up is usually enough.

75
Getting news
How do you usually get your news?

Almost entirely online, through my phone. It's quick and I can check headlines whenever I have a spare moment. I rarely watch the news on television any more, which feels outdated to me now.

76
Keeping memories
Do you like keeping photos of special events?

Definitely. I love looking back at photos from trips and celebrations, because they bring the feelings of that day rushing back. They're some of my most treasured possessions, honestly.

77
Public events
Do you enjoy going to public events?

I do, in moderation. Cultural fairs and festivals have a wonderful atmosphere, and I like being part of the crowd. Very large, chaotic events can be a bit much for me, though, so I pick and choose.

78
Comedy
Do you like watching comedy shows?

Yes, I love a good laugh at the end of a long day. Comedy is the easiest way for me to switch off. I especially enjoy clever, observational humour that plays on everyday situations.

79
Streaming
Do you use streaming services?

All the time. They've completely transformed how I watch things. Having a huge library available whenever I want is incredibly convenient, and I honestly can't imagine going back to fixed TV schedules.

80
Culture and young people
Do you think young people are losing interest in traditional culture?

To some extent, yes, mainly because of global influences and social media. But I don't think it's diminishing entirely. Many young people still take great pride in their roots, just alongside a more modern lifestyle.

Category 5 — Daily Life & Opinions

81
Weather
What kind of weather do you like?

I'm fondest of mild, sunny weather, the kind you get here in winter. It's warm enough to be comfortable but not stifling, so you can actually enjoy being outside. The intense summer heat I find rather oppressive.

82
Seasons
What is your favourite season?

Winter, easily. It's short here but lovely, with cool, pleasant days and clear skies. It's the season when I feel most energetic and want to be out and about, so I always look forward to it.

83
Transport
How do you usually travel around?

Mostly by bus and rickshaw for daily trips, and occasionally a ride-share app when I'm in a hurry. It works well enough, though the traffic can turn even a short journey into a real ordeal.

84
Traffic
Is traffic a problem where you live?

Very much so, especially in the bigger cities. Congestion during rush hour is genuinely frustrating and eats into everyone's day. I think better public transport is really the only viable long-term solution.

85
Weekends vs weekdays
Do you prefer weekdays or weekends?

Weekends, without question. Weekdays are productive but tiring, whereas weekends give me space to rest and see people. That balance is what keeps me going through the week.

86
Making plans
Do you like making plans?

I do, generally. Having a rough plan gives me direction and stops me wasting time. That said, I try not to over-plan, because some of the best moments come from being a little spontaneous.

87
Productivity
Are you more productive in the morning or the evening?

Definitely the morning. My mind is sharpest then, so I tackle my hardest tasks early. By the evening my focus fades, so I save lighter activities for later in the day.

88
Change
Do you welcome change in your life?

Cautiously, yes. I'm comfortable with routine, but I've learned that a bit of change usually leads to growth. So even when it feels daunting at first, I try to see it as an opportunity rather than a threat.

89
Decisions
Are you good at making decisions?

I'd say I'm decisive with small things, but I take my time over big ones, weighing up the options carefully. Occasionally I deliberate for too long, but I'd rather be thorough than rush and regret it.

90
Patience
Are you a patient person?

Reasonably, yes, though it depends on the situation. I'm quite patient with people, but I can get restless when I'm stuck waiting, in traffic for instance. It's something I'm consciously working on.

91
Colours
Do you have a favourite colour?

I do, it's blue. I find it calming and easy on the eye, so a lot of my clothes and even my room lean towards it. It has a soothing quality that I'm naturally drawn to.

92
Lucky numbers
Is there a number you consider lucky?

Not really, to be honest. I'm not very superstitious about numbers. I know many people have a lucky one, but I've never felt that any particular number has made a difference in my life.

93
Giving gifts
Do you enjoy giving gifts?

I really do. I love the challenge of finding something that suits the person perfectly, and their reaction when they open it is the best part. A thoughtful gift means far more than an expensive one.

94
Receiving gifts
Do you like receiving gifts?

Of course, who doesn't? But what I appreciate most is the thought behind it. A small, meaningful present from someone who knows me well means much more than something lavish given without much care.

95
Letters and emails
Do you often write emails or letters?

Emails, yes, mostly for work. Handwritten letters have almost disappeared, which is a shame. There's a personal warmth to a letter that a quick email just can't quite replicate.

96
Calling or texting
Do you prefer phone calls or text messages?

For quick things, texting, since it's fast and doesn't interrupt anyone. But for anything important, I much prefer a call. You can hear the tone of someone's voice, which makes the exchange feel far more genuine.

97
Being organised
Are you an organised person?

Fairly, yes. I like to keep things in order, both my space and my schedule, because clutter genuinely stresses me out. A little organisation up front spares me a lot of hassle later.

98
Helping others
Do you enjoy helping other people?

I really do. My whole job is essentially about helping people reach their goals, and I find that deeply fulfilling. Even small acts of help brighten my day, and they usually come back around in some form.

99
Talking to strangers
Do you find it easy to talk to strangers?

It depends on the setting, but generally yes. I'm fairly comfortable striking up a conversation, and you often learn something interesting. A little friendliness goes a long way in putting people at ease.

100
Happiness
What kinds of things make you happy?

Simple things, mostly. Time with people I love, a good book, a quiet evening after a productive day. I've realised over time that lasting contentment rarely comes from big events, but from small, everyday moments.
