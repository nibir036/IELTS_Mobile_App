  • and how you felt about it

One of the hardest decisions I have faced was turning down a stable job to pursue something more uncertain but closer to what I actually wanted. This was about two years ago, just after I had been offered a comfortable position with good pay. On paper it was the sensible choice, which is exactly what made walking away so agonising. Telling my family was nerve-wracking, since they naturally worried, but they came round once they saw how determined I was. Looking back, it was a real turning point, and although the following months were rocky, it taught me that the most rewarding paths are rarely the most comfortable. I still feel it was the right call.
