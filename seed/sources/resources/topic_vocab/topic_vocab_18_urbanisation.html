Urbanisation & Housing

IELTS Academic topic vocabulary — Topic 18. Terms, meanings, and model collocations for Writing and Speaking.

TermMeaning / UsageExample collocation
urbanisationthe growth of towns as people move from rural areasRapid urbanisation strains city services.
urbanrelating to a town or cityUrban areas are growing faster than rural ones.
ruralrelating to the countrysideYoung people leave rural villages for work.
metropolisa large, busy cityThe metropolis draws migrants from across the region.
metropolitanrelating to a large city and its suburbsThe metropolitan area spans several districts.
megacitya city with over ten million peopleSeveral Asian megacities keep expanding.
suburba residential area on a city's edgeMany families settle in the suburb.
suburbanrelating to the suburbsThe suburban sprawl keeps spreading outward.
inner citythe central, often poorer area of a cityThe inner city suffers from neglect.
city centrethe busy commercial heart of a cityRents are highest in the city centre.
districtan area of a city with a distinct characterThe financial district empties at night.
neighbourhooda local area where people liveThe neighbourhood has a strong community spirit.
residentialdesigned for people to live inThe zone is strictly residential.
commercialused for business and tradeThe street is lined with commercial premises.
industrialrelating to factories and manufacturingThe old industrial zone is being redeveloped.
housingbuildings where people live, collectivelyAffordable housing is in short supply.
accommodationa place for someone to live or stayStudents struggle to find accommodation.
dwellinga place of residenceThousands of new dwellings are planned.
tenanta person who rents a propertyThe tenant signed a one-year lease.
landlorda person who rents property to othersThe landlord raised the rent again.
rentmoney paid to use a propertySoaring rent pushed families out of the centre.
mortgagea loan used to buy a homeHigh rates made the mortgage hard to afford.
affordabilityhow far people can pay for somethingHousing affordability has worsened sharply.
propertyland or buildings that are ownedProperty prices keep climbing.
real estateland and buildings as a marketInvestors poured money into real estate.
infrastructurethe basic systems a city needs to functionAgeing infrastructure struggles to cope.
amenitiesuseful features that make life easierThe estate lacks basic amenities.
utilitiesessential services like water and powerNew homes must be connected to utilities.
sanitationsystems for clean water and wastePoor sanitation spreads disease.
sluma crowded, poor urban areaMillions still live in a slum.
shanty towna settlement of makeshift homesA shanty town grew on the city's edge.
informal settlementunplanned housing without legal titleAn informal settlement sprang up overnight.
overcrowdingtoo many people in too little spaceOvercrowding strains sanitation and schools.
congestionsevere crowding, especially of trafficTraffic congestion paralyses the centre.
population densitythe number of people per unit of areaThe city has a very high population density.
sprawlthe uncontrolled spread of a cityUrban sprawl swallowed the farmland.
expansionthe act of growing larger in areaRapid expansion outpaced planning.
developmentthe building of new structures on landA huge development rose on the waterfront.
redevelopmentrebuilding a run-down areaThe docks underwent major redevelopment.
regenerationbringing new life to a declining areaUrban regeneration revived the old quarter.
renovationthe repair and improvement of a buildingThe terrace needs full renovation.
gentrificationwealthier people moving into a poorer areaGentrification displaced long-time residents.
displacementforcing people to leave their homesRising rents cause widespread displacement.
urban planningdesigning the layout and growth of citiesGood urban planning eases congestion.
zoningrules dividing land by permitted useZoning keeps factories away from homes.
land usethe way land is developed or managedEfficient land use limits sprawl.
high-risea tall building with many floorsNew high-rise flats dominate the skyline.
skyscrapera very tall buildingThe skyscraper towers over the district.
tower blocka tall residential buildingFamilies were housed in a tower block.
densitythe degree to which an area is built upHigher density reduces travel distances.
green spaceopen land with grass and trees in a cityResidents want more green space.
public spaceopen areas for everyone to useWell-kept public space builds community.
pedestriana person travelling on footThe square was made pedestrian-only.
liveablepleasant and comfortable to live inThe city ranks among the most liveable.
sustainable citya city designed to limit environmental harmPlanners aim for a sustainable city.
smart citya city using technology to run efficientlySensors make the smart city more efficient.
commuteto travel regularly between home and workMany commute for hours each day.
migrationthe movement of people between areasRural-to-urban migration continues apace.
influxa sudden arrival of many peopleThe influx overwhelmed local housing.
depopulationa decline in the number of residentsVillages face steady depopulation.
rural exoduspeople leaving the countryside in large numbersA rural exodus emptied the farms.
urban decaythe decline of a once-thriving city areaUrban decay blighted the old mills.
deprivationa lack of basic needs or comfortsPockets of deprivation persist downtown.
povertythe state of being extremely poorUrban poverty is often hidden.
homelessnessthe condition of having no homeRising rents fuelled homelessness.
social housinghousing provided at low cost by the stateWaiting lists for social housing are long.
subsidisedsupported by money from the governmentThe scheme offers subsidised rents.
tenurethe terms on which a home is heldSecure tenure gives families stability.
evictionthe legal removal of a tenantThe family faced sudden eviction.
squattingliving in an empty building without permissionSquatting rose as rents climbed.
dilapidatedin a state of disrepairThe block was left dilapidated for years.
derelictabandoned and in poor conditionA derelict warehouse stood on the corner.
renewalthe process of making an area new againUrban renewal transformed the harbour.
facilitiesbuildings or services for a particular useThe estate needs better facilities.
transport networkthe system of routes for moving peopleA strong transport network links the suburbs.
connectivityhow well places are linked togetherPoor connectivity isolates outer districts.
liveabilitythe quality of being good to live inGreen space improves urban liveability.
vibrantfull of life and activityThe quarter has a vibrant street life.
bustlingfull of busy movementThe market is always bustling.
cosmopolitanmade up of people from many culturesThe capital is strikingly cosmopolitan.
segregationthe separation of groups within a cityHousing segregation deepened inequality.
integrationthe mixing of different groups in a communityMixed estates encourage integration.
communitythe people living in one areaRegeneration can rebuild a community.
relocationmoving people or activities elsewhereThe scheme funded relocation of residents.
occupancythe use of a building by residentsThe tower reached full occupancy.
vacantempty and not in useMany flats stand vacant as investments.
mixed-usecombining homes, shops, and officesThe mixed-use block houses cafés and flats.
urban sprawlthe spread of a city into the countrysideUrban sprawl raises commuting times.
overpopulationtoo many people for the available resourcesOverpopulation strains urban water supplies.
affordable housinghomes priced within reach of ordinary peopleThe city pledged more affordable housing.
