Food & Diet

IELTS Academic topic vocabulary — Topic 17. Terms, meanings, and model collocations for Writing and Speaking.

TermMeaning / UsageExample collocation

nutritiousfull of nourishing valueA nutritious breakfast fuels the day.
dietthe kinds of food a person usually eatsA balanced diet includes many food groups.
balanced dieta diet with the right mix of nutrientsA balanced diet is key to good health.
caloriea unit measuring the energy in foodThe snack is high in calories.
proteina nutrient needed to build the bodyBeans are a good source of protein.
carbohydratea nutrient that gives the body energyRice is rich in carbohydrate.
vitamina substance in food vital for healthCitrus fruit provides plenty of vitamin C.
minerala natural substance the body needsMilk supplies calcium, an important mineral.
fibreplant material that aids digestionWhole grains are high in fibre.
ingredienta component used in a dishFresh herbs are the key ingredient.
cuisinea style of cooking from a regionThai cuisine balances many flavours.
flavourthe taste of a food or drinkThe spices add a rich flavour.
texturethe feel of food in the mouthThe bread has a crusty texture.
appetitea desire for foodThe walk gave him a hearty appetite.
portionthe amount of food served to one personLarge portions encourage overeating.
servinga single measured amount of foodAim for five servings of fruit a day.
staplea basic food eaten regularlyRice is a staple across much of Asia.
producefresh fruit and vegetablesThe market sells local produce.
organicgrown without artificial chemicalsOrganic vegetables cost more to produce.
processed foodfood altered from its natural stateProcessed food is often high in salt.
additivea substance added to foodSome additives prolong shelf life.
preservativea substance that keeps food freshThe snack contains artificial preservatives.

refinedprocessed to remove natural partsRefined sugar offers little nutrition.
wholesomegood for health and wellbeingThey favour simple, wholesome meals.
junk foodfood high in fat, sugar, or salt and low in valueToo much junk food harms health.
fast foodquickly prepared, often unhealthy foodFast food is cheap but calorie-dense.
takeawaya meal bought to eat elsewhereThey ordered a takeaway after work.
home-cookedprepared at home rather than boughtA home-cooked meal is usually healthier.
vegetarianeating no meat or fishShe has followed a vegetarian diet for years.
veganeating no animal products at allA vegan diet excludes dairy and eggs.
plant-basedbased mainly on foods from plantsPlant-based eating is gaining ground.
obesitythe condition of being very overweightObesity rates are rising worldwide.
overweightweighing more than is healthyPoor diets leave many children overweight.
malnutritionpoor health from a lack of proper foodMalnutrition still affects millions.
deficiencya lack of something neededA vitamin deficiency can cause fatigue.
consumptionthe eating or using of foodSugar consumption has soared.
overeatto eat more than the body needsIt is easy to overeat rich food.
moderationavoiding excess in eating or drinkingEnjoy treats in moderation.
indulgeto allow oneself a pleasureThey indulge in dessert on weekends.
craveto feel a strong desire for foodMany people crave sweet snacks.
digestto break down food in the bodyFibre helps the body digest food.

hydrationkeeping the body supplied with waterGood hydration supports concentration.
seasoningsalt, herbs, or spices added to foodA little seasoning lifts the dish.
spicean aromatic substance used to flavour foodThe curry uses a blend of spices.
recipeinstructions for preparing a dishShe followed her grandmother's recipe.
culinaryrelating to cookingThe city is a culinary destination.
gourmetof high quality, for fine tastesThe restaurant serves gourmet dishes.

appetisinglooking or smelling good to eatThe spread looked thoroughly appetising.
savourysalty or spicy rather than sweetHe prefers savoury snacks.
blandlacking strong flavourThe soup was watery and bland.
nourishingproviding what is needed for healthA nourishing stew warms the body.
perishablelikely to decay quicklyDairy is a perishable product.
preserveto keep food from spoilingSalting helps preserve fish.
spoilagethe decay of foodRefrigeration slows spoilage.
food securityreliable access to enough safe foodClimate change threatens food security.
faminean extreme and widespread shortage of foodDrought triggered a severe famine.
scarcitya shortage of somethingWater scarcity affects crop yields.

sustainableable to continue without harming the futureSustainable farming protects the land.
agriculturethe practice of farmingModern agriculture feeds billions.
cultivationthe growing of cropsRice cultivation needs plenty of water.
harvestthe gathering of ripe cropsA good harvest eased food prices.

hygienepractices that keep food clean and safeStrict hygiene prevents contamination.

additive-freecontaining no added chemicalsThe label claims the juice is additive-free.
dietaryrelating to dietSome follow strict dietary rules.
palatea person's sense of tasteTravel broadened her palate.
comfort foodfood that gives a sense of wellbeingSoup is her favourite comfort food.
consumeto eat or drink somethingPeople now consume more sugar than ever.
ediblesafe or suitable to be eatenOnly part of the plant is edible.
nutritional valuethe worth of a food for healthCrisps have little nutritional value.
supplementa product taken to add nutrientsShe takes a daily vitamin supplement.
farm-to-tablefood served soon after being grownThe menu follows a farm-to-table ethos.
seasonalavailable at a certain time of yearSeasonal fruit is fresher and cheaper.

overindulgenceexcessive eating or drinkingFestive overindulgence is common.
well-balancedcontaining a good mix of nutrientsA well-balanced meal sustains energy.
