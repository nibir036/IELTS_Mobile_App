Travel & Tourism

IELTS Academic topic vocabulary — Topic 9. Terms, meanings, and model collocations for Writing and Speaking.

TermMeaning / UsageExample collocation
tourismthe business of attracting and serving visitorsTourism is a major source of foreign currency.
destinationa place people travel toThe island became a popular holiday destination.
itinerarya planned route and schedule for a tripWe mapped out a packed itinerary for the week.
excursiona short trip, usually for pleasureA guided excursion took us to the ruins.
expeditionan organised journey with a clear purposeThe expedition crossed the desert on foot.
voyagea long journey, especially by seaThe voyage across the ocean took three weeks.
getawaya short holiday away from routineA weekend getaway helped them unwind.
backpackingtravelling cheaply with a rucksackShe spent a gap year backpacking through Asia.
sightseeingvisiting notable places as a touristWe spent the morning sightseeing in the old town.
landmarka famous building or feature that is easily recognisedThe tower is the city's most famous landmark.
attractiona place people visit for interest or enjoymentThe museum is the region's top attraction.
heritagevalued traditions and sites passed down over timeThe site was granted World Heritage status.
cultural immersiondeep involvement in a local way of lifeHomestays offer genuine cultural immersion.
off the beaten trackaway from the usual tourist routesWe sought villages off the beaten track.
picturesquevisually charming, like a pictureThe picturesque harbour draws many photographers.
scenicproviding attractive natural viewsWe took the scenic route along the coast.
breathtakingastonishingly beautiful or impressiveThe view from the summit was breathtaking.
vibrantfull of energy and lifeThe market has a vibrant atmosphere at night.
bustlingfull of busy activityThe bustling streets are packed with vendors.
remotefar from towns and hard to reachThe lodge sits in a remote mountain valley.
accommodationa place to stay while travellingWe booked budget accommodation near the station.
hospitalityfriendly, generous treatment of guestsThe locals were known for their warm hospitality.
resorta place designed for holidays and relaxationThe seaside resort fills up every summer.
hostelcheap shared lodging, popular with travellersBackpackers often stay in a hostel.
check-inthe process of registering on arrivalCheck-in opens three hours before the flight.
reservationan arrangement to keep a place or seatI made a reservation at a riverside hotel.
vacancyan available room or spaceThe inn had no vacancy during the festival.
package holidaya trip with travel and lodging sold togetherA package holiday can work out cheaper.
all-inclusivecovering meals, drinks, and activities in the priceThe all-inclusive deal suited the family.
tour operatora company that organises and sells tripsThe tour operator handled all the bookings.
travel agencya business that arranges travel for clientsA travel agency secured our visas and flights.
departurethe act of leaving on a journeyOur departure was delayed by bad weather.
arrivalthe act of reaching a destinationOn arrival, we were met by a local guide.
layovera short stop between flightsWe had a six-hour layover in transit.
jet lagtiredness from crossing time zonesSevere jet lag kept her awake for days.
passportan official document for international travelRenew your passport well before you travel.
visaofficial permission to enter a countryA tourist visa allows a stay of ninety days.
customsthe authority that checks goods at bordersWe had nothing to declare at customs.
immigrationthe checkpoint controlling entry to a countryThe queue at immigration was unusually long.
currency exchangeswapping one country's money for another'sThe airport currency exchange offers poor rates.
travel insurancecover against losses or emergencies abroadTravel insurance covered the cancelled flight.
souveniran object kept as a reminder of a placeShe bought a small souvenir from each city.
mementoa keepsake recalling a person or eventThe ticket became a treasured memento.
peak seasonthe busiest, most expensive time to travelPrices soar during peak season.
off-seasona quieter period with lower pricesTravelling in the off-season saves money.
high seasonthe period of greatest tourist demandHotels raise rates in the high season.
tourist trapa place that overcharges visitorsThe restaurant turned out to be a tourist trap.
mass tourismtravel by very large numbers of visitorsMass tourism has strained the city's resources.
overtourismtoo many tourists harming a destinationOvertourism is eroding fragile heritage sites.
ecotourismresponsible travel that conserves natureEcotourism funds the protection of the reserve.
sustainable tourismtravel that limits long-term harmSustainable tourism balances growth and conservation.
carbon footprintthe greenhouse gases a journey producesFlying greatly increases a traveller's carbon footprint.
cultural exchangethe sharing of customs between peoplesTravel fosters genuine cultural exchange.
authenticgenuine and true to local characterWe searched for an authentic local meal.
commercialisationturning something into a source of profitCommercialisation has diluted the festival's charm.
hospitality industrybusinesses serving travellers and guestsThe hospitality industry employs thousands locally.
infrastructurethe facilities that support travel and servicesPoor infrastructure deters visitors to the region.
amenitiesuseful features that add comfortThe hotel offers modern amenities.
cuisinea style of cooking from a regionThe city is famed for its street cuisine.
delicacya prized or unusual local foodGrilled seafood is the island's great delicacy.
wanderlusta strong desire to travelA map on the wall fed her wanderlust.
globetrottera person who travels widelyA seasoned globetrotter, he has seen fifty countries.
nomada person who moves from place to placeRemote work created a class of digital nomads.
expatriatea person living outside their native countryA large expatriate community settled in the port.
commuteto travel regularly between home and workMany commute long distances into the capital.
transitthe act of passing through on the way elsewhereWe stayed overnight in transit.
embarkto begin a journey or board a vesselPassengers embark an hour before sailing.
ventureto go somewhere risky or unfamiliarFew travellers venture into the interior.
exploreto travel through in order to discoverWe set out to explore the coastline.
roamto move about without a fixed routeWe spent the day free to roam the old quarter.
stopovera brief stay during a longer journeyA two-day stopover let us see the capital.
boarding passa document allowing you onto a planeKeep your boarding pass ready at the gate.
terminala building where journeys begin and endThe new terminal handles millions of passengers.
domesticwithin one's own countryDomestic flights rose as borders stayed shut.
internationalbetween or involving different countriesInternational travel resumed once rules eased.
repatriationreturning someone to their home countryThe embassy arranged the repatriation of stranded tourists.
seasonaldepending on the time of yearThe resort relies on seasonal trade.
revenueincome earned from tourismTourism revenue props up the local economy.
economic dependenceheavy reliance on one source of incomeEconomic dependence on tourism leaves the island exposed.
seasonal employmentwork available only part of the yearMany rely on seasonal employment in resorts.
conservationprotection of nature and heritageEntry fees help fund conservation of the park.
preservationkeeping something in its original stateStrict rules ensure the preservation of the site.
degradationdecline in the quality of a place or environmentHeavy footfall has caused visible degradation.
crowdedfull of too many peopleThe beaches grow unbearably crowded in July.
tranquilpeaceful and calmWe found a tranquil lake away from the crowds.
rejuvenateto make someone feel fresh and energetic againA quiet retreat can rejuvenate the mind.
broaden horizonsto widen one's experience and understandingTravel broadens horizons and challenges assumptions.
culture shockdisorientation from an unfamiliar cultureFirst-time travellers often feel culture shock.
staycationa holiday spent at or near homeRising costs made a staycation appealing.
