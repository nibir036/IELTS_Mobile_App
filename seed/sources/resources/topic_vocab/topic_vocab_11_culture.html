Culture & Tradition

IELTS Academic topic vocabulary — Topic 11. Terms, meanings, and model collocations for Writing and Speaking.

TermMeaning / UsageExample collocation
culturethe shared customs, beliefs, and arts of a peopleFood is a vivid expression of a nation's culture.
traditiona custom passed down over generationsThe festival keeps an ancient tradition alive.
customa usual way of behaving in a societyRemoving shoes indoors is a local custom.
rituala set of actions performed in a fixed wayThe ceremony follows a centuries-old ritual.
ceremonya formal event marking an occasionA graduation ceremony honours years of study.
heritagevalued traditions and achievements inherited from the pastThe old city is part of the world's heritage.
legacysomething handed down from earlier generationsThe empire left a lasting cultural legacy.
ancestryone's family and cultural originsMany celebrate their mixed ancestry.
identitythe characteristics that define a person or groupLanguage is central to cultural identity.
ethnicitybelonging to a group with shared culture or descentThe city is home to many ethnicity groups.
indigenousnative to and originating in a placeIndigenous peoples hold deep ties to the land.
folklorethe traditional stories and beliefs of a peopleLocal folklore is rich with sea legends.
mythologya body of traditional mythsThe paintings draw on ancient mythology.
folkrelating to the traditional culture of ordinary peopleFolk music varies greatly from region to region.
cuisinea characteristic style of cookingEach province has its own distinctive cuisine.
costumetraditional dress of a region or eraDancers wore elaborate traditional costume.
attireclothes worn for a particular occasionGuests arrived in formal attire.
artefactan object made by humans, often historicalThe museum displays rare ancient artefacts.
craftsmanshipskill in making things by handThe carpets show superb craftsmanship.
handicrafta skilled handmade object or activityThe village is famous for its handicraft.
cultural diversitythe presence of many different culturesCultural diversity enriches a society.
multiculturalismthe coexistence of several cultures in one placeMulticulturalism defines the modern metropolis.
cosmopolitanincluding people and influences from many countriesThe city has a cosmopolitan atmosphere.
assimilationadopting the culture of a dominant groupRapid assimilation can erode minority customs.
integrationbecoming part of a wider society while keeping identitySuccessful integration respects both cultures.
acculturationcultural change from contact between groupsAcculturation blends old and new practices.
cultural exchangethe sharing of ideas and customs between groupsStudent programmes encourage cultural exchange.
cross-culturalinvolving two or more culturesCross-cultural understanding reduces conflict.
cultural heritagetraditions and sites inherited from the pastWars threaten priceless cultural heritage.
preservationkeeping something safe from loss or decayPreservation of dialects is a growing concern.
conservationprotection of valued places and traditionsGrants support the conservation of temples.
revivala renewal of interest in an old practiceThere has been a revival of folk dance.
renaissancea period of renewed cultural flourishingThe region enjoyed an artistic renaissance.
continuitythe unbroken passing on of traditionsFestivals provide a sense of continuity.
extinctionthe complete dying out of a practice or languageSeveral languages face extinction.
erosionthe gradual weakening of somethingGlobalisation accelerates the erosion of local customs.
homogenisationthe process of becoming uniform and alikeCultural homogenisation narrows diversity.
westernisationadopting Western culture and valuesRapid westernisation changed urban tastes.
globalisationincreasing interconnection of the world's culturesGlobalisation spreads trends across borders.
cultural identitythe sense of belonging to a cultureMigration can complicate cultural identity.
normsaccepted standards of behaviour in a groupSocial norms differ sharply between cultures.
valuesthe principles a culture holds importantRespect for elders is a core value.
beliefsomething accepted as true by a groupShared belief binds a community together.
superstitiona belief based on tradition rather than reasonOld superstition still shapes some choices.
etiquettethe rules of polite social behaviourDining etiquette varies from culture to culture.
protocolaccepted formal rules for an occasionDiplomatic protocol governs state visits.
taboosomething forbidden by customCertain topics remain a social taboo.
conventiona widely accepted way of doing thingsGreeting with a bow is a local convention.
rite of passagea ceremony marking a life stageThe ceremony is a rite of passage into adulthood.
festivityjoyful celebration of an occasionThe streets filled with festivity.
festivala day or period of cultural celebrationThe harvest festival draws visitors nationwide.
commemorateto honour the memory of a person or eventThe holiday commemorates the nation's founding.
observeto follow a custom or holidayMany families still observe the old rites.
symbolicrepresenting a deeper meaningThe red dress carries symbolic meaning.
emblema symbol standing for a group or ideaThe lotus is a national emblem.
embodyto express or represent an idea in physical formThe dance embodies the community's spirit.
ancestralrelating to one's forebearsThey returned to their ancestral village.
time-honouredrespected because of long useWeaving here is a time-honoured craft.
deep-rootedfirmly established over a long timeThe belief is deep-rooted in rural life.
long-standinghaving existed for a long timeIt is a long-standing custom of the region.
quaintattractively old-fashionedThe town retains a quaint charm.
authenticgenuine and true to originVisitors seek an authentic cultural experience.
indigenous knowledgetraditional understanding held by native peoplesIndigenous knowledge aids modern conservation.
oral traditionculture passed on by word of mouthHistory survived through oral tradition.
dialecta regional form of a languageEach valley speaks its own dialect.
vernacularthe everyday language of ordinary peoplePoetry in the vernacular reached a wide audience.
multilingualable to use several languagesA multilingual society values many tongues.
stereotypea fixed, oversimplified image of a groupTravel can break down cultural stereotypes.
prejudicean unfair opinion formed in advanceEducation helps reduce prejudice.
ethnocentrismjudging others by one's own cultureEthnocentrism blocks real understanding.
cultural relativismjudging a culture by its own standardsCultural relativism urges openness to difference.
toleranceacceptance of beliefs different from one's ownReligious tolerance underpins social harmony.
coexistenceliving together peacefully despite differencesThe region models peaceful coexistence.
harmonypeaceful agreement and balanceDiverse groups live in harmony here.
cultural appropriationadopting elements of another culture inappropriatelyDebate surrounds cultural appropriation in fashion.
diffusionthe spread of cultural traits between groupsTrade routes drove cultural diffusion.
fusiona blend of different cultural elementsThe music is a fusion of East and West.
hybridsomething combining two distinct influencesThe cuisine is a hybrid of several traditions.
melting pota place where cultures blend togetherThe port city is a cultural melting pot.
pilgrimagea journey to a sacred placeThe annual pilgrimage draws millions.
sacredregarded with deep religious respectThe grove is a sacred site to the people.
secularnot connected with religionThe state maintains a secular school system.
devoutdeeply religious in practiceThe devout observe the fast each year.
generationalrelating to or shared across age groupsSome customs face a generational decline.
nostalgiasentimental longing for the pastOld songs stir a sense of nostalgia.
modernitythe condition of being modernTradition and modernity often sit in tension.
adaptto adjust to new conditionsCustoms adapt as societies change.
perpetuateto make something continue indefinitelyStories perpetuate a community's values.
instilto establish an idea gradually in someoneParents instil cultural pride in children.
cultural capitalknowledge and tastes that confer social advantageAccess to the arts builds cultural capital.
