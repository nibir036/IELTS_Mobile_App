Science & Research

IELTS Academic topic vocabulary — Topic 14. Terms, meanings, and model collocations for Writing and Speaking.

TermMeaning / UsageExample collocation
researchcareful study to discover new factsYears of research led to the breakthrough.
hypothesisa proposed explanation to be testedThe hypothesis was confirmed by the data.
theorya well-supported explanation of phenomenaThe theory explains how species adapt.
experimenta test done to investigate a questionThe experiment was repeated to confirm the result.
methodologythe system of methods used in a studyA sound methodology underpins good research.
analysisdetailed examination of data or elementsStatistical analysis revealed a clear trend.
datafacts and figures collected for studyThe team gathered data over five years.
evidencefacts that support a conclusionThere is strong evidence for the claim.
findinga result discovered through researchThe key finding surprised the field.
conclusiona judgment reached after reasoningThe study drew a cautious conclusion.
variablea factor that can change in an experimentResearchers controlled every variable.
correlationa mutual relationship between two thingsThe data showed a strong correlation.
causationthe relationship where one thing causes anotherCorrelation does not prove causation.
samplea small group studied to represent a wholeA larger sample improves reliability.
control groupa group not given the treatment, for comparisonThe control group received a placebo.
placeboa dummy treatment used as a comparisonThe placebo had no active ingredient.
reliabilitythe degree to which results can be trustedRepeated tests confirmed the data's reliability.
validityhow well a study measures what it claims toFlawed design undermined the study's validity.
accuracycloseness to the true valueNew instruments improved measurement accuracy.
precisionthe degree of exactness in measurementThe experiment demanded great precision.
biasa distortion that skews results unfairlyResearchers guarded against bias.
replicateto repeat a study to check its resultsOther labs failed to replicate the finding.
peer reviewevaluation of research by other expertsThe paper passed rigorous peer review.
empiricalbased on observation and experimentThe claim rests on solid empirical data.
qualitativebased on qualities rather than numbersInterviews provided rich qualitative data.
quantitativebased on measurable quantitiesThe survey yielded useful quantitative data.
observationthe act of noting and recording factsCareful observation revealed the pattern.
phenomenonan observable fact or eventScientists study the phenomenon closely.
discoverythe finding of something previously unknownThe discovery reshaped the whole field.
breakthrougha sudden important advanceA medical breakthrough saved countless lives.
innovationa new method, idea, or productConstant innovation drives the industry.
inventiona newly created device or processThe invention transformed daily life.
advancementprogress or improvementRapid advancement marked the decade.
technologythe application of science to practical tasksNew technology accelerated the research.
laboratorya place equipped for scientific workThe samples were analysed in the laboratory.
apparatusequipment used for a scientific purposeThe apparatus measured tiny changes.
specimena sample taken for studyEach specimen was carefully labelled.
triala test of something, especially a treatmentThe drug entered its final clinical trial.
clinical triala controlled study of a medical treatmentThe clinical trial involved thousands of patients.
simulationan imitation of a process by a modelA computer simulation predicted the outcome.
modela simplified representation of a systemThe climate model forecasts warming.
parametera limit or factor defining a systemThey adjusted a single parameter.
statisticsthe study and use of numerical dataThe paper relied heavily on statistics.
significantlarge enough to be meaningful, not by chanceThe difference was statistically significant.
margin of errorthe range within which a result may varyThe poll had a small margin of error.
anomalysomething that deviates from the normThe anomaly prompted further study.
benchmarka standard against which things are measuredThe test set a new benchmark for safety.
criteriona standard by which something is judgedEach sample met the inclusion criterion.
assumptionsomething accepted as true without proofThe model rests on a key assumption.
interpretationan explanation of the meaning of resultsTheir interpretation of the data differed.
implicationa likely consequence suggested by findingsThe results carry major implications.
applicationa practical use of a discoveryThe theory found wide application in medicine.
scrutinyclose and critical examinationThe claims faced intense scrutiny.
rigorousextremely thorough and carefulThe study used a rigorous design.
systematicdone according to a fixed plan or systemThey took a systematic approach to testing.
objectivenot influenced by personal feelingsScience strives to be objective.
subjectivebased on personal opinion or feelingSelf-reports can be highly subjective.
hypothesiseto put forward an idea to be testedScientists hypothesise a hidden cause.
investigateto examine something carefullyThe team set out to investigate the effect.
examineto inspect closely and in detailThey examine each sample under a microscope.
demonstrateto show clearly with evidenceThe trial demonstrated the drug's safety.
verifyto check that something is trueIndependent labs verified the result.
validateto confirm that something is soundFurther tests validated the method.
refuteto prove a claim wrongNew data refute the old theory.
disproveto show that something is falseThe experiment aimed to disprove the idea.
substantiateto provide evidence to support a claimStudies substantiate the health benefits.
quantifyto express or measure as a numberIt is hard to quantify the long-term effect.
estimateto roughly calculate a valueResearchers estimate the figure at ten percent.
calibrateto adjust an instrument for accuracyTechnicians calibrate the sensors daily.
synthesiseto combine parts into a wholeThe review synthesises dozens of studies.
geneticsthe study of heredity and genesAdvances in genetics reshaped medicine.
biotechnologytechnology based on biologyBiotechnology promises new therapies.
moleculethe smallest unit of a chemical compoundThe drug targets a single molecule.
organisman individual living thingThe organism adapted to the heat.
ecosystema community of organisms and their environmentPollution disrupts the whole ecosystem.
particlea tiny piece of matterPhysicists study subatomic particles.
radiationenergy emitted as waves or particlesShielding blocks harmful radiation.
catalystsomething that speeds up a reaction or changeThe grant acted as a catalyst for research.
reactiona process in which substances changeThe chemical reaction released heat.
interdisciplinarycombining two or more fields of studyThe project took an interdisciplinary approach.
cutting-edgeat the most advanced stage of developmentThe lab uses cutting-edge equipment.
pioneeringbeing among the first to explore somethingHer pioneering work opened a new field.
ethicsmoral principles governing conductResearch must respect strict ethics.
ethicalmorally right and properThe study raised difficult ethical questions.
fundingmoney provided for a projectThe lab competes for research funding.
granta sum of money awarded for researchThe team won a major government grant.
scholara person engaged in advanced studyA visiting scholar joined the department.
dissertationa long essay based on original researchShe defended her doctoral dissertation.
publicationa published piece of researchThe lab has many high-impact publications.
citationa reference to a source in researchThe paper drew hundreds of citations.
groundbreakingintroducing new and important ideasThe groundbreaking study changed policy.
