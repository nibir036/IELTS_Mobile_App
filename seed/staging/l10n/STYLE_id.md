# Indonesian style guide for app content (IELTS AI by nextED)

Same idea as the Bangla version (STYLE_bn.md): **Indonesian explanation, English examples**. The reader is an
Indonesian IELTS student, often weak in English. Write EASY, natural Bahasa Indonesia — standard (baku) but
friendly, like a good tutor explaining in class, not stiff textbook language.

## Voice
- Address the student as "Anda", short clear sentences. Re-say the idea naturally; never word-for-word.
- Prefer everyday words: "mudah" not "sederhana sekali", "salah" not "keliru" when both fit, "perlu" not
  "diperlukan adanya".

## Keep in English (never translate)
- IELTS / exam terms students see on the paper or use in class: passage, paragraph, question, option, keyword,
  paraphrase, synonym, distractor, trap, skim, scan, True / False / Not Given, Yes / No / Not Given, Matching
  Headings, Summary Completion, examiner, band, evidence, topic sentence, word limit, NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS,
  grammar terms (subject, verb, article, tense, clause …), Task 1 / Task 2, Part 1 / 2 / 3, cue card, fluency …
  Use them as English words in the Indonesian sentence; attach -nya with a hyphen when needed ("keyword-nya");
  for plurals use "semua option" / "beberapa paragraph", not "option-option".
- Everything quoted from the passage, the question text, option text, answers, example sentences, model answers
  and paragraph letters: keep them in English exactly as given, inside the same quotation marks.
- Option letters (A, B, C…), paragraph letters, question numbers, years and figures stay as they are.
- Brand names: IELTS AI by nextED.

## Important: it must really be Indonesian
Indonesian uses the same alphabet as English, so a sentence left in English is easy to miss. Every explaining
sentence must be Indonesian; only the kept English terms, quotes and examples stay English.

## Example of the target style
> Passage menyebut "wasted space" — inilah paraphrase dari "an area of little value" di option B. Option D
> membicarakan masa sekarang, bukan twentieth century. Pekerja pemeliharaan memang disebut di passage, tetapi
> passage tidak pernah mengatakan bahwa atap itu berbahaya (C).

## Bangla-specific parts
Some lessons were written for Bangla speakers (e.g. "Bangla speakers often say…", Bangla example sentences in
Bengali script, "Bangla logic" boxes). Translate them faithfully: "Bangla" stays the language being described
("bahasa Bangla", "penutur bahasa Bangla"), every Bengali-script example is kept exactly as it is, and only the
explanation around it is translated. Exception: where "Bangla" simply means the student's own mother tongue — "restate the task in your own words (in Bangla)", "think in Bangla first", "explanations are in Bangla", "its Bangla meaning" next to a word list now glossed in Indonesian — write Indonesian instead. Do not rewrite them for Indonesian speakers.

## Accuracy
- Keep every fact and every reason of the English original; add nothing new, drop nothing.
- If the English says why a wrong option is wrong, the Indonesian must say it too.
- Keep **bold** markers around the matching words.
