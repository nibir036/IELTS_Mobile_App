# Nepali style guide for app content (IELTS AI by nextED)

Same idea as the Bangla version (STYLE_bn.md): **Nepali explanation, English examples**. The reader is a Nepali
IELTS student, often weak in English. Write EASY, everyday standard Nepali (देवनागरी), the way a good teacher in
Kathmandu explains to a student.

## Voice
- Respectful "तपाईं" form, short clear sentences. Re-say the idea naturally in Nepali, never word-for-word.
- Prefer common words over Sanskrit-heavy ones: "सजिलो" not "सरल" when both fit, "गल्ती" not "त्रुटि",
  "चाहिन्छ" not "आवश्यकता पर्दछ".
- Use । (purnabiram) as the full stop in Nepali sentences; ? and ! as usual.

## Keep in English (never translate)
- IELTS / exam terms students see on the paper or use in class: passage, paragraph, question, option, keyword,
  paraphrase, synonym, distractor, trap, skim, scan, True / False / Not Given, Yes / No / Not Given, Matching
  Headings, Summary Completion, examiner, band, evidence, topic sentence, word limit, NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS,
  grammar terms (subject, verb, article, tense, clause …), Task 1 / Task 2, Part 1 / 2 / 3, cue card, fluency …
  Attach Nepali endings with a hyphen: passage-मा, paragraph-को, option-हरू, keyword-लाई, answer-चाहिँ.
- Everything quoted from the passage, the question text, option text, answers, example sentences, model answers
  and paragraph letters: keep them in English exactly as given, inside the same quotation marks.
- Option letters (A, B, C…), paragraph letters, question numbers, years and figures from the source stay as they are.
- Brand names: IELTS AI by nextED.

## Numbers in Nepali prose
Use Devanagari digits for counts and steps in your own sentences (२ वटा option, ३ वटा चरण, १० मिनेट), but never
change numbers that come from the passage, the question, the answer or an English example.

## Example of the target style
> Passage-मा "wasted space" भनिएको छ — यही option B-को "an area of little value"-को paraphrase हो। D ले अहिलेको
> समयको कुरा गर्छ, twentieth century-को होइन। Maintenance worker-हरूको कुरा passage-मा छ, तर roof खतरनाक थियो भनेर
> कतै भनिएको छैन (C)।

## Bangla-specific parts
Some lessons were written for Bangla speakers (e.g. "Bangla speakers often say…", Bangla example sentences in
Bengali script, "Bangla logic" boxes). Translate them faithfully: "Bangla" stays the language being described
("बंगाली", "बंगाली भाषीहरू"), every Bengali-script example is kept exactly as it is, and only the explanation around
it is translated. Exception: where "Bangla" simply means the student's own mother tongue — "restate the task in your own words (in Bangla)", "think in Bangla first", "explanations are in Bangla", "its Bangla meaning" next to a word list now glossed in Nepali — write Nepali instead. Do not rewrite them for Nepali speakers.

## Accuracy
- Keep every fact and every reason of the English original; add nothing new, drop nothing.
- If the English says why a wrong option is wrong, the Nepali must say it too.
- Keep **bold** markers around the matching words.
