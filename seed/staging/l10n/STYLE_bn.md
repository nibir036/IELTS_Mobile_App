# Bangla style guide for app content (IELTS AI by nextED)

Write exactly like sir's guides (seed/sources/guides/IELTS_Writing_Complete_Book_Bangla.pdf and the
Speaking guide): **Bangla explanation, English examples**. The reader is a Bangladeshi IELTS student.

## Voice
- Formal-friendly "আপনি" form, short clear sentences, like a good teacher talking. No robotic, word-for-word
  translation: re-say the idea naturally in Bangla.
- Standard Bangla spelling (চলিত ভাষা). Use । as the full stop in Bangla sentences.

## Keep in English (never translate)
- IELTS / exam terms students see on the paper or use in class: passage, paragraph, question, option, keyword,
  paraphrase, synonym, distractor, trap, skim, scan, True / False / Not Given, Yes / No / Not Given, Matching
  Headings, Summary Completion, examiner, band, evidence, topic sentence, word limit, NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS …
  Attach Bangla case endings with a hyphen like the guides: passage-এ, paragraph-টা, option-গুলো, keyword-এর.
- Everything quoted from the passage, the question text, option text, answers and paragraph letters:
  keep them in English exactly as given, inside the same quotation marks. E.g. "wasted space" → "an area of little value".
- Option letters (A, B, C…), paragraph letters, question numbers, years and figures from the passage stay as they are.

## Numbers in Bangla prose
Use Bangla digits for counts and steps in your own sentences (২টি option, ৩টি ধাপ, ১০ মিনিট), but never change
numbers that come from the passage, the question or the answer.

## Examples of the target style (from sir's Writing guide)
> ছোটখাটো slip, যেমন teh / recieve — এগুলোকে বলে "slip of the pen"। খুব বেশি না হলে এগুলো তেমন ক্ষতি করে না।
> কিন্তু যে বানান ভুলে শব্দের অর্থই পাল্টে যায়, সেটা examiner ধরেন "ভুল শব্দ ব্যবহার" হিসেবে — এটা অনেক বেশি serious।

> Tip: লেখা শুরুর আগে প্রশ্নের মূল noun-গুলো দেখে প্রতিটির দুটো করে synonym question paper-এ লিখে রাখুন।

For a reading explanation, the target looks like:
> Passage-এ বলা হয়েছে "wasted space" — এটাই option B-র "an area of little value"-এর paraphrase। D বর্তমান সময়ের
> কথা বলছে, twentieth century-র নয়। Maintenance worker-দের কথা passage-এ আছে, কিন্তু কোথাও বলা নেই যে roof বিপজ্জনক ছিল (C)।

## Accuracy
- Keep every fact and every reason of the English original; add nothing new, drop nothing.
- If the English says why a wrong option is wrong, the Bangla must say it too.
