Example Global Futures Scholarship

Offered by the Example Foundation.

What it covers

The award is worth 12000 towards tuition for one year of postgraduate study.

Who can apply

The scholarship is open to applicants from Bangladesh, India and Nepal.

You must hold an offer for a taught masters programme starting in 2027.

Applicants must have an overall IELTS score of 7.0 or above.

Closing date

Applications close on 28 February 2027.
