MSc Artificial Intelligence

School of Computing, Example University

Course overview

This one year full-time MSc covers machine learning, computer vision and natural language processing. Students complete a research dissertation in the summer term.

Entry requirements

You will normally need a good honours degree in a computing or mathematical subject.

Applicants need an overall IELTS score of 6.5 with no component below 6.0.

Fees

Tuition for 2027 entry is £24,500 per year for international students.

How to apply

The deadline for applications is 15 January 2027. Applications received after this date will be considered only if places remain.

Entry is for the September 2027 intake.
