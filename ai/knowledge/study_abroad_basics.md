# Study abroad basics with nextED

## How nextED helps
nextED counsellors help students from Bangladesh choose a country, course and university, prepare applications, find scholarships and get visa-ready. The office is in Banani, Dhaka.

## Choosing a country
Popular destinations are the UK, Canada, Australia, the USA, Germany, Ireland, Malaysia and Japan. Compare total cost (tuition plus living), course length, post-study work rights, and the IELTS score usually asked for. A one-year UK Masters is often cheaper overall than a two-year Masters elsewhere, but post-study work rules differ by country and change often - always check the official government page.

## IELTS
Most universities ask for IELTS Academic. Undergraduate courses commonly ask for 6.0 overall, postgraduate 6.5, with no band below 5.5 or 6.0. Some courses (law, medicine, teaching) ask for 7.0 or more. Visa rules may also set a minimum. A higher score widens the choice of universities and scholarships.

## Typical documents
Passport, academic transcripts and certificates, IELTS result, statement of purpose, CV, two references, proof of funds (bank statements and sponsor letters), and for some countries a tuberculosis test. Translations must be certified.

## Timeline
Start 9-12 months before the intake. September/autumn intakes have the most choice; January/spring intakes exist in the UK, Canada and Australia. Scholarship deadlines are often 6-10 months before the course starts.

## Scholarships
Big government awards (Chevening, Commonwealth, Australia Awards, DAAD) are very competitive and need leadership evidence. University awards are easier to get and often automatic for good grades. A strong IELTS score and early application raise the chances.
